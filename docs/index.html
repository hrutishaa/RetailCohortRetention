Retail Cohort Retention

Monthly acquisition cohorts, retention, LTV and RFM segments for a UK online gift retailer, Dec 2009 to Nov 2011. Built from SQL over the UCI Online Retail II dataset.

Cohort retention heatmap

Share of each first-purchase cohort that ordered again N months later. December 2009 is the starting customer base, not true new customers.

Average retention curve (new-customer cohorts)

Weighted by cohort size; each month only uses cohorts old enough to have reached it.

Active customers per month: new vs returning

Returning customers carry most of the volume, and new-customer intake shrank in 2011.

Cumulative revenue per customer (LTV) by acquisition quarter

GBP per acquired customer after 0, 3, 6 and 12 months. Cohorts Jan to Nov 2010.

RFM segments (as of 1 Dec 2011)

Quintile scores on recency, order count and spend.

Data cleaning log

Rows removed by each rule, in the order the rules run (see sql/01_clean.sql).
